# Development instructions

## Required project guidance

Product requirements and engineering guidelines live in the
[`llm-wiki/`](llm-wiki/) knowledge base. Before changing an area of the project,
read and follow the relevant linked guidance:

- [Product overview](llm-wiki/wiki/maps/product-overview.md)
- [Development guidelines](llm-wiki/wiki/operations/development-guidelines.md)
- [Technology stack](llm-wiki/wiki/architecture/technology-stack.md)
- [HTTP API map](llm-wiki/wiki/maps/http-api.md)
- [Testing guidelines](llm-wiki/wiki/operations/testing-guidelines.md)

## LLM wiki

The LLM wiki connects approved product intent to architecture, implementation,
and test evidence. Start with [the wiki home](llm-wiki/Home.md) or
[the wiki index](llm-wiki/wiki/index.md).

Before adding or changing requirements, implementation evidence, or related wiki
documentation, read and follow
[`llm-wiki/WIKI_AGENTS.md`](llm-wiki/WIKI_AGENTS.md), the authoritative wiki
maintenance contract.
